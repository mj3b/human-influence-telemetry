# v0.6.5 Audit Plan

The audit serves one decision: which mapped H1 through H9 and paper claims may enter a conclusion. The executable run validates the map schema, resolves evidence locators, recomputes five gates, closes dependencies, detects eight controlled corruptions, checks E5 claim boundaries, and regenerates the publication figure.

The expected terminal state is `PASS_WITH_EXCEPTIONS`. A `FAIL` state blocks release when a declared gate differs from computation, generated output is stale, a negative control escapes detection, or paper prose crosses a protected empirical boundary.

The responsible author reviews claim support and fitness. The audit supplies deterministic consistency checks and records that author review. Independent reproduction remains an open exception.
