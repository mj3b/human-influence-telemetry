## Release function

Version 0.6.5 adds research-publication controls around the preserved HIT artifact. It maps H1 through H9 and four material paper claims to exact evidence, five gates, five evidence-fitness dimensions, dependencies, limitations, reversal conditions, and conclusion eligibility.

The generated audit runs eight negative controls and E5 claim-boundary checks. `PASS_WITH_EXCEPTIONS` means the controls behave as declared while every claim with failed or indeterminate required evidence remains blocked.
